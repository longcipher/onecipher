Deployless ERC-6492 simulation payload is
`data = bytecode || abi.encode(signer, hash, signature)` with `to` omitted
(eth_call-only simulation; nothing is ever deployed).

This is a canonical public deployment artifact (like a well-known contract
address), vendored as data so the ERC-6492 verifier needs no codegen
dependency. The verification *logic* (magic dispatch, chain-id pre-check,
strict bool decoding) is implemented in `src/siwx_verify.rs` in this
workspace's own style over the existing `HpxRpcClient`.

It is not the Ambire UniversalSigValidator singleton compiled with salt 0x0.
